# Talking to users by hand (conversion plan, Phase 5)

With a few hundred users, ten conversations teach more than any dashboard.
This is the founder's job, not a cron's: send these yourself, from your own
inbox (or WhatsApp where you have a number), one person at a time.

## Who

Admin → Analytics → **Download outreach list (CSV)**. Each person is in one
segment, the first that fits:

| Segment | Who | What you want to learn |
|---|---|---|
| `heavy` | The 10 accounts that have run the most video | What they make, what's missing, what they'd pay |
| `nearly_out` | Free accounts that ran AutoClip and have ≤5 minutes left | What stops them paying now |
| `hit_limit` | Shown the "over your plan's limit" offer (recorded from 26 Nov) | How big and long their videos really are |
| `payer` | Everyone who has paid | Why they paid; what nearly stopped them |

## What to offer

In exchange for a 15-minute call or a reply to three questions, pick one:

- a free **100 Clip Minutes** (Admin → Users → the user → Adjust minutes, reason
  "outreach 2026-11"), or
- **50% off the first month** of Creator or Pro (a one-use coupon from Admin →
  Coupons).

## The message

Keep it short and from you, not from "the Clipiro team". Replace the brackets.

> **Subject:** Quick question about your clips
>
> Hi [first name],
>
> I'm [your name], I built Clipiro. I saw you've used AutoClip on
> [N minutes of video / your GTA streams], and I'd love to hear how it went.
>
> Could you answer three quick questions (or jump on a 15-minute call)?
>
> 1. What were you trying to make, and where were you going to post it?
> 2. Where did Clipiro fall short?
> 3. If it did what you wanted, what would you pay for it each month, and how
>    would you pay (UPI, card)?
>
> As a thank-you I'll add 100 free Clip Minutes to your account (or 50% off
> your first month, if you'd rather).
>
> Thanks,
> [your name]

For **payers**, swap the questions for: *what made you decide to pay, what
nearly stopped you, and what would make you upgrade or stay?*

## Writing it down

Keep one line per person: segment, answer to each question, and the price they
named. After ten, look for the same answer three times: that's the next thing
to build or fix. Review with the funnel numbers on 10 Dec 2026.
